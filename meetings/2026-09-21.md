### Working Groups Updates

Join: [https\://github.com/Safety-Critical-Rust-Consortium/safety-critical-rust-consortium/issues/new?template=membership.yml](https://github.com/Safety-Critical-Rust-Consortium/safety-critical-rust-consortium/issues/new?template=membership.yml) 

* Safety Critical Rust Consortium   
  * In-person meeting on 2026-09-07 was good, we had a number of action items come out of this  
    * extraction of helpful pieces of things like the Rustonomicon and Unsafe Code Guidelines into other normative docs like the Reference and FLS; alignment and possible future merger of Reference and FLS  
    * sharing of more success stories on our website, e.g. Sonair, which have gotten a sort of "mid-grade" level of safety-certification of their sensor for IEC 61508  
    * snagging a new domain (safetycriticalrust.org), rather than our existing one arewesafetycriticalyet.org as this kind of tongue-in-cheek wording has become problematic for members of the SCRC who also do GUI work due to areweguiyet.com  
  * Coding Guidelines Subcommittee  
    * Have seen one Automotive OEM begin [contributing](https://github.com/Safety-Critical-Rust-Consortium/safety-critical-rust-coding-guidelines/issues/1234) to the Safety-Critical Rust Coding Guidelines  
    * Spoke with an Automotive supplier that's done their own internal coding standard and it seems there's interest to become more involved with our open effort for coding guidelines  
  * Tooling Subcommittee  
    * We have now obtained the safety standards and will work on a mechanism to share them in a controlled manner where members cannot download them and we grant access.  
    * We've shared some work on Project Goals in the past perhaps here, but here's updates about [the Safety-Critical Rust roadmap](https://goals.rust-lang.org/2026/roadmap-safety-critical-rust.html)  
      * the FLS continues to be maintained,  
      * there are PRs out for MC/DC,  
      * we have done some sharing of a library we have unsafe/unsoundness questions about to t-opsem,  
      * and we've coordinated a bit to get the OK from the Clippy team to land lints related to the Safety-Critical Rust Coding Guidelines
